Multi-objective modeling of larger ecosystems
============

The mathematical method used in MIMEco has been used previously to model ecosystems of more than two organisms (specifically 3 in budinich et. al [1], and 5 and 11 in Lambert et. al. [2])

MIMEco is currently specialized for paiwise ecosystems to allow its automatized and replicable inference of the interaction score, type, and exchanged metabolites. 
Indeed, scaling to larger ecosystems makes these inference more challenging, and requires a more hands-on analysis.

While facilitating this larger scale analysis and including it in MIMEco is an objective of or team, 
it is still possible for experienced users to slightly modify utils functions of MIMEco to do such an analysis in the meantime.



[1] Budinich, Marko, et al. "A multi-objective constraint-based approach for modeling genome-scale microbial ecosystems." PloS one 12.2 (2017): e0171744.


[2] Lambert, Anna, et al. "Community metabolic modeling of host-microbiota interactions through multi-objective optimization." Iscience 27.6 (2024).
